---
title: Scrap holds its nerve
date: 2026-08-04
summary: Ferrous steadies after a soft spring; aluminium spreads narrow; a word on weighbridge discipline.
---

Ferrous has steadied. HMS into the usual export destinations found a floor in July, and August has opened with buyers back at the table, if not yet reaching across it. Shredded commands its customary premium; plate and structural moves without argument. Yards holding material through the soft spring have mostly been vindicated, which will make them braver next time — worth remembering.

Aluminium spreads have narrowed. Clean extrusion offcuts remain the easiest sale on the board, castings want careful description, and UBC is a market for those who enjoy paperwork. Cast iron does what cast iron does: steady demand, steady money, no romance.

A word on discipline. Every dispute we have seen this quarter began at a weighbridge, and every one was avoidable with tickets issued, photographed and shared at loading. The desk supplies weighbridge documentation on every lifting as a matter of course. It is cheaper than argument.
